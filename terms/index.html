PacePot is a free savings planning tool. These terms explain what it is, what it is not, and what you are responsible for. Plain English, no small print.

Last updated 20 August 2026.

PacePot is a planning tool, not advice

PacePot helps you set savings targets and work out the monthly pace needed to reach them. It is not financial advice, investment advice, mortgage advice, legal advice or tax advice, and nothing on this site should be treated as a personal recommendation.

The calculators are deliberately simple. They divide what you still need by the time you have left. They do not model interest, investment growth, inflation, tax or charges, and they cannot account for your wider circumstances. Where a decision matters, speak to a qualified adviser.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the figures you enter and the decisions you make. PacePot works from what you type in, so a wrong figure produces a confident wrong answer.
